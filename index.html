MOLTEN ALUMINUM
POURING THE MOLD
SAND CASTING
01

Melt

Aluminum ingots and clean scrap are melted in the furnace at around 700 °C.

02

Pour

Molten metal is poured into sand or permanent molds shaped like each kadai, handi or part.

03

Cool & release

Castings cool and set, then are broken out of the mold and trimmed.

04

Grind & polish

Every piece is ground, polished and inspected before handles and finishes go on.
